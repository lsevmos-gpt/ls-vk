# Cinematic plausibility lens — Clean

Inspiration: https://github.com/niiithish/seedance-clean (unofficial mirror attributing concepts to Higgsfield Academy). Redistribution terms of original material are not verified. This is an original project-local summary, not verbatim source or a claim of endorsement.

Write what the viewer should physically SEE or HEAR, not loose adjectives like "epic" or "premium". Establish: opening frame composition, where the human stands/looks, precise motion, credible inertial weight and contact, physically sensible scene geography, a limited purposeful camera move, plausible lens perspective, source/direction/falloff of light, restrained color and realistic ending.

For real people preserve original identity, skin pores, asymmetry, catchlights, clothing folds, body proportions and natural muscle dynamics. Do not beautify, morph, reshape faces/bodies, invent tattoos, fabricate limbs, add hyper-smooth wax/plastic skin or mutate gym equipment. Preserve genuine architecture and camera path when source footage exists.

For brand video, exact lettering, URLs, prices and logos should be original assets or added in post-production when reliability matters. Do not fabricate a logo from prose.

This is a cross-model film-craft review for both 2.0 and 2.5, not permission to claim particular output format, resolution, fps or support for an interface feature.
